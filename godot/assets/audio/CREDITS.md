The Freesound files are the HQ preview encodes of the original uploads, which
Freesound publishes under the same CC0 licence. The Kenney packs are CC0 by
design.

`engine_loop.ogg` was raised 10 dB and `music_race.ogg` lowered 8 dB so the
engine sits under the music and the music sits under the effects. `crash.ogg`
and `slingshot.ogg` are small mixes of the listed sources, made with ffmpeg.
