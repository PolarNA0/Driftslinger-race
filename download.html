What is in it

  1. One button. Hold to latch onto a pole and carve a tight arc; release to fire off straight along the tangent.
  2. The road is straights joined by circular arcs, so a corner's pole is the centre of the arc. A clean line always exists.
  3. Score is distance in metres. Going faster only comes from wasting less of each corner.
  4. Your best distance is saved on the device, per road.
